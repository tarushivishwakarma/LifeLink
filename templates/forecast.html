Analytical Model Specifications

Demand Trajectory

Computed using a first-order damped polynomial fit over the last 28 days of issuance data with conservative slope damping (capped at 15% of baseline) to avoid overshooting.

Inflow & Wastage Assumption

Inflow assumes a 14-day rolling mean collection rate; expiration rate is calculated from a 28-day moving average.

Classification

Deterministic time-series statistical modeling (NumPy/Pandas). Not machine learning or black-box inference.
