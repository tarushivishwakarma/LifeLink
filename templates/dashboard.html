Forecasting Methodology & Transparency Notice

LifeLink applies mathematical time-series modeling rather than unverified black-box algorithms. Future 14-day demand estimates use a damped linear regression trend (NumPy polyfit) computed from the preceding 28 days of hospital issuance, bound by dynamic slope clipping to avert artificial runaway predictions. Inflow predictions reflect 14-day rolling mean donation rates. All records shown are synthetic academic simulations.
